PETTRACK INFORMATION @if (publicPage === 'about') {

About PetTrack

PetTrack helps pet owners and authorized relocation teams organize local pet journeys in the Philippines. It keeps pet profiles, route checklists, submitted documents, schedules, and manually updated milestones together.

PetTrack coordinates records and communication. It does not transport animals, issue permits, certify veterinary documents, or provide live GPS tracking.
} @else if (publicPage === 'how') {

How it works

  1. An administrator creates your account.
  2. Add a pet profile and submit a Philippine local relocation request.
  3. Review the configured route checklist and submit requested documents.
  4. Authorized staff review submissions, coordinate schedules, and update the relocation milestones.

Checklist items are administrative references. Confirm current requirements with the relevant authorities and transport provider.

} @else if (publicPage === 'support') {

Contact support

For account access, relocation coordination, or document review questions, contact your organization’s PetTrack administrator through its approved support channel.

PetTrack does not provide emergency veterinary or animal transport services. For urgent concerns, contact your veterinarian or relevant local authority.
} @else {

Privacy at a glance

PetTrack limits records to authenticated users with an operational need. Pet owners see their own pets and relocation records; staff see requests authorized for their work; administrators manage system records.

Documents are stored privately and served only after a server-side access check. Do not submit information that is not needed to coordinate your pet’s relocation.

Backup and recovery

Back up the PetTrack database and private document directory together to an access-controlled location. A database dump alone does not include uploaded files.

  1. Take a database backup and a matching copy of server/storage/documents.
  2. Encrypt backups at rest and limit access to authorized operators.
  3. For recovery, stop application writes, restore both from the same backup point, then verify permissions and a sample document.
  4. Resume service only after confirming the database and file store match.

Scheduled pickup and document-expiration reminders run through scripts/send-pickup-reminders.php. Configure Windows Task Scheduler or cron to run it daily. Email and SMS require a separately configured provider.
